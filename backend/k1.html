About the Marketplace

The NITC Marketplace is a student-led initiative to provide a reliable peer-to-peer exchange platform for buying, selling, and renting items and services within the NIT Calicut campus.

Whether you're looking to sell your used books, find a roommate, or report a lost item — this is the one-stop hub built exclusively for the campus community.

Vision

To foster a connected and resourceful student community through safe and accessible exchange systems.

Mission

To empower students by creating a trusted platform for marketplace and resource exchange within the NIT Calicut ecosystem.
